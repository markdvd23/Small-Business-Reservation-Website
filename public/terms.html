Before you book

Terms.

Reservations

A reservation is confirmed when a booking number appears. Arrive on time; tables may be released 15 minutes after the booked time. Branch availability may change for safety or operational reasons.

Cancellations

You may cancel an upcoming reservation from your account. For urgent or same-day changes, contact the branch directly.

Accounts

Provide accurate information, protect your password, and do not misuse the service. Accounts or bookings involved in abuse, fraud, or disruption may be restricted.

Menu and pricing

Availability, pricing, promotions, and branch hours may change. Confirm special dietary needs with branch staff.

Service availability

The website may be unavailable during maintenance or network interruption. Contact the branch when an urgent booking issue cannot be handled online.
