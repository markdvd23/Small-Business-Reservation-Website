Your information

Privacy.

We collect only what is needed to manage your account, reservation, pickup hold, or order.

What we keep

Name, email address, optional mobile number, booking details, order details, account role, and operational audit records.

How we use it

To confirm and manage bookings, contact you about a visit, operate branches, prevent abuse, and improve service. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see it

You can see your own account and tickets. Authorized staff can see booking details needed for service. Managers can review operational audit records.

Your choices

You may correct your display name and cancel eligible reservations from your account. Contact the business to request a copy or deletion of your information where legally permitted.

Retention and security

Records are retained only as needed for operations, safety, accounting, and legal obligations. Firebase authentication and database access rules protect account data.
